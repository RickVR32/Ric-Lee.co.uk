About

I'm Ric — a solo developer and technical builder based in London.

I started building software to solve my own problems. That grew into UMNi, then PsyTectrics, alongside commercial projects for other businesses — the kind of range that's usually split across an agency's departments, run instead by one person who actually does the work.

Today I work across software, web development, automation, and infrastructure, with a particular interest in systems where different technologies have to work together. I like understanding the whole picture — not just the interface, not just the code, the system.

  • Studio: Ric-Lee Digital Developments
  • Response time: within 24 hours, working days
  • Delivery: direct — no subcontracting, no markup

02

Where it started

Built for myself first.

Most of what I know came from building things I needed. UMNi began as my own algorithmic trading infrastructure, and PsyTectrics as a behavioural assessment platform. Both had to actually work, every day, with real data, which is a hard teacher.

That experience is what I bring to client projects: websites, booking systems, payments and automation built to the same standard as my own products. You can see what that looks like on the work page.
