Returns & Refunds Policy

Plain-language version: you can cancel before work starts and get your money back. Once I've started, you pay for the work already done and no more. If something I deliver is faulty or isn't what we agreed, I fix it, and if I can't, you get a refund.

Cancelling

Who this covers

This policy applies to anything bought from Ric-Lee Digital Developments (Ric Lee, sole trader, London): the Launch, Brand and Build packages, deposits, invoices, the Care Plan, and design work. If you've agreed a written quote for a specific project, that quote takes priority over anything here where the two differ.

Your 14-day right to cancel (individuals)

If you're buying as an individual, not for a business, you have 14 days from booking to cancel a service without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. I won't start work in that period unless you ask me to, and I'll ask you to confirm that by email. If you ask me to start early and then cancel within the 14 days, you pay for the work done up to the point you told me, in proportion to the full price, and I refund the rest. Once a service has been fully delivered, the right to cancel ends, but only if you asked me to start within the 14 days and I've told you this in advance.

Business customers

If you're buying for a business, the statutory 14-day right doesn't apply, but I follow the same approach: cancel before work starts and you get a full refund, and cancel part-way and you pay only for the work done.

How to cancel

Send a message through the contact form or email hello@ric-lee.co.uk saying what you're cancelling. A clear statement is enough; there is no form to fill in. I'll confirm by reply and tell you what, if anything, is owed either way.

By package

Launch (£750, paid in full)

Cancel before I start work: full refund. Cancel after I've started: you get back the price less the work already done, worked out as a fair share of the package against what has been completed (logo, site build, domain and policy documents). I'll send the breakdown before refunding anything.

Brand (£1,500, £450 deposit to book)

The £450 deposit is refunded in full if you cancel before I start work. Once work has begun, the deposit covers the work done so far; if that work is worth less than the deposit, the difference is refunded, and if it's worth more, I'll tell you the figure before invoicing anything further. The remaining balance is invoiced separately and isn't due for work I haven't done.

Build (from £2,500, scoped per project)

Build projects are priced after a call and agreed in writing, including the payment stages. Cancellation and refunds follow that written quote. If it doesn't say otherwise, the same rule applies: you pay for work completed up to the point of cancelling.

Care Plan (from £25 / month)

You can cancel at any time by telling me. It stops at the end of the period you've already paid for. Part-periods already started aren't refunded, because hosting, backups and updates for that period have already been provided.

Design, merch and print-on-demand

Mockups, logos and brand kits are digital deliverables, and revisions within the agreed scope are included. Physical items made to your design (printed products, for example) are made to order, so the 14-day right to cancel doesn't apply once production has started. If an item arrives faulty, damaged or not as ordered, tell me within 30 days of delivery with a photo and I'll replace it or refund it.

Third-party costs

Costs I've paid to someone else on your behalf, such as domain registration or paid licences, can't be refunded once bought. I'll always tell you about them before I buy.

Faulty or unsatisfactory work

What you can expect

Services are carried out with reasonable care and skill, and what I deliver should match what we agreed. This is your legal right under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and I treat it the same way for business customers.

If something's wrong

Tell me what's wrong within 30 days of delivery. If the work doesn't match the agreed scope, or something doesn't work as it should, I'll put it right at no charge. If I can't put it right in a reasonable time, you're entitled to a price reduction or a refund for the affected work. Changes that go beyond what we agreed are new work and are quoted separately.

Not covered

Problems caused by changes made by someone else after handover, by third-party services I don't control (hosting outages, payment-provider changes), or by content you supplied are outside this policy, though I'll usually help you sort them out.

How refunds work

Timing

Once a refund is agreed, I process it within 14 days. It goes back to the card or payment method you used, through Stripe, and can take a few working days to show on your statement after that.

Amount

Refunds are in pounds sterling and are calculated as described above. Where something is owed for work already done, I'll send a short itemised breakdown first so you can see how the figure was reached.

Disagreements

If we can't agree, get in touch and I'll talk it through directly. You can also use Citizens Advice for free guidance, and your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by anything on this page.

Questions about any of this?

Send a message and I'll get back to you directly — no forms, no ticket numbers.
