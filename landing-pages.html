What's included

Scope of work

The baseline for a single-page campaign build — scoped further per project.

  • Single-page structure built around one clear call to action
  • Design matched to the campaign, launch, or existing brand
  • Fast-loading, mobile-first build — most campaign traffic arrives on a phone
  • Enquiry, sign-up, or checkout-link forms wired straight to email
  • Analytics-ready structure for tracking what a paid campaign is doing
  • Domain and hosting set up end to end, or deployed under an existing domain
  • Basic on-page SEO where it's relevant to the campaign
