What's included

Scope of work

Every project is scoped individually — this is the baseline for application work.

  • Booking, quoting, and payment workflows — Stripe Checkout, dynamic payment links generated on approval
  • Password-protected admin dashboards — manage bookings, pricing, customers, and stats yourself
  • Member and supplier portals, including token-based no-login access where it removes friction
  • Relational databases (Postgres/Supabase) designed and built around the actual workflow, not bolted on after
  • Automated, idempotent client communications — confirmations, reminders, scheduled reports, all logged
  • Custom scoring, calculation, or matching engines where the business logic is genuinely bespoke
  • Third-party API integrations — payments, AI-generated content, messaging
  • Care Plan available for ongoing hosting & updates
